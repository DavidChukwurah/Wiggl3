First time you open it

What it does

  • Moves the cursor across your screens only when you have been idle, every 15 seconds to 4 minutes (your choice).
  • Runs for as long as you say: until you turn it off, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or between two times you pick.
  • Keeps the screen awake while it is on.
  • Lives in the tray or menu bar; closing the window keeps it running. One copy at a time.
